# Code of conduct

MXJ AUDIO is built by people who give their time for free. Be kind.

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content.
- Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who break these rules.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

*Türkçe: Herkese saygılı ve sabırlı ol. Fikirleri ve kodu eleştir, kişileri değil. Taciz, hakaret ve
ayrımcılığa yer yok. Kurallara uymayanlar engellenebilir.*
